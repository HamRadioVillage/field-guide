### IMPROVISED ANTENNAS

1. Cantenna (for "can it ham?"):
  a. https://www.changpuak.ch/electronics/cantenna.php
  b. A number 303 can (think campbell's soup) is 81mm in diameter and 111mm long. This recipe will work for 2968Mhz.
  c. Place a 7/32 hole in a number 303 can at 37mm from closed end
  d. Strip 25.3mm (just under 1in) of coax, exposing the inner conductor
  e. Strip outer sheath (not braided shield) an additional 37mm (from start of exposed inner conductor). Tape end of braid to prevent fraying.
  f. Insert coax into can until outer sheath touches edge of hole. Ensure it is as straight as possible. Secure. (can should be electrically connected to braid)
  g. strip and terminate coax as close to can as possible.
