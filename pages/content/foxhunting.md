# RADIO FOXHUNTING — BASICS

1. For long range (>100m), use YAGI antenna.
2. Slowly sweep across horizon until signal strongest. This is direction of fox.
3. Move to second location (move perpendicular, over a long(ish) distance), repeat sweep to find new direction.
4. Where directions intersect is rough location of fox.
5. For short range (<100m), switch to LOOP antenna.
6. Rotate until signal is weakest (deepest null). This is direction of fox.
8. Use horizontal sweep or walk towards null.
NOTE: Some foxes move.
NOTE: Use attenuator to decrease antenna sensitivity when close to fox.
