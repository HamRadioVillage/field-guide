# IMPROVISED ANTENNAS

1. Cantenna ("can it ham?")
   - Ref:
     - https://www.changpuak.ch/electronics/cantenna.php
   - Example (303 can):
     - Diameter: 81 mm  
     - Length: 111 mm  
     - Tuned ~2968 MHz
   - Build:
     - Drill 7/32" hole at 37 mm from closed end
     - Strip coax:
       - 25.3 mm inner conductor exposed
       - +37 mm outer sheath removed (leave braid intact)
       - Tape braid to prevent fray
     - Insert coax:
       - Outer sheath flush with hole
       - Keep probe straight
       - Ensure can electrically connects to braid
     - Terminate coax:
       - Keep feedline as short as possible

2. Slim Jim
   - Ref:
     - https://m0ukd.com/calculators/slim-jim-and-j-pole-calculator/
   - This build is for ~145 MHz
   - Cut length of "lamp wire" (two conductors, flat) to 152cm
   - From one end, mark wire at 5.5cm. Strip 0.5cm from both wires, keeping insulation between them intact.
   - Solder 50ohm coax outer shield to one wire, inner core to the other. Lengths of conductor on coax should be the same. Terminate as close to joint as possible.
   - From end near coax termination, measure to 50.2cm. From this point, cut and remove 2cm section of wire connected to shield of coax.
   - On both ends of wire: separate the two strands for 2cm along their seam, strip 1cm from each wire, then turn the wires towards each other and solder them together.
   - Hang in a tree (straight, vertical)
   - For J-pole, remove long section of shield-side wire and trim 0.5cm from remaining loose wire.

3. Dipole
   - This build is for ~145 MHz
   - Strip 49.2cm of outer sheath of coax.
   - At base of stripped section, gently separate braid enough to pull out center connector. Ensure shield length is same as center conductor.
   - Insulate braided shield with electrical tape (or original sheathing)
   - Orient shield and center conductor such that they lay in a straight line facing opposite each other. Secure so they maintain a straight line.
   - Terminate intact end of coax as close to split as possible.

4. Loop
   - cut a 90cm section of coax
   - remove a 2cm section of the outer sheath and braid from the middle. Insulate and reinforce with tape.
   - Terminate one end normally
   - Terminate other end without center conductor
   - Connect both ends to a Tee
   - Sharp null through center of loop

6. Yagi
   - Notes:
     - Highly directional (gain + front/back rejection)
     - Used for direction finding and range
